We collect only what we need to take, deliver and support your order. We do not sell your personal information.

Who we are

Legacy 7 is operated by EZlancing, based in Amman, Jordan. This policy explains what personal information we collect through this website and when you order from us, and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Order details: your name, phone number, delivery address and the items you order.
  • Messages: the content of messages you send us, for example on WhatsApp.
  • Payment information: when you pay by card, the payment is handled by a third-party payment provider. We do not receive or store your full card details.

How we use your information

  • To confirm, prepare and deliver your order.
  • To contact you about your order, returns or support requests.
  • To keep the records we are required to keep by law.

Who we share it with

We share your information only with the services needed to complete your order: delivery companies, payment providers and the messaging service you use to contact us. We do not sell or rent your personal information to anyone.

Cookies and tracking

This website does not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. Images, video and other files on the site are served by third-party hosting providers, which may receive technical information such as your IP address when your browser loads them. If we add analytics or advertising tools in future, we will update this policy.

How long we keep it

We keep order records for as long as needed to provide support and to meet our legal and accounting obligations, and delete them when they are no longer needed.

Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it where we are not required to keep it. Contact us using the details below.

Children

This website is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.
