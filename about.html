About Us

Last updated: 12 November 2026

The Last Dance Collectibles makes framed collector's pieces for football fans who want more than a poster on the wall.

What we make

Each The Last Dance Collectibles piece is a 12×16 inch shadow box that tells a career as a timeline, with custom-illustrated collectible plates and gold foil accents inside a solid frame. The Icon Series is our first collection.

How it arrives

Every piece ships in a reinforced box with mounting hardware and a desk stand included, so it can go on display the day it arrives.

Who we are

The Last Dance Collectibles is a product line by EZlancing, based in Amman, Jordan. Our pieces are original fan collectibles and are not official merchandise of any player, club or tournament.
