Return and Refund Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

  • You can request a return within 14 days of delivery.
  • The item must be unused and in its original packaging.
  • Report damaged or wrong items within 48 hours of delivery, with photos.
  • Approved refunds are issued within 7 to 10 business days.

Returns

If you are not satisfied with your purchase, contact us within 14 days of the delivery date to request a return. To be accepted, the item must be unused, undamaged and in its original packaging with all included hardware.

How to request a return

  • Message us on WhatsApp with your name, the phone number used for the order and the reason for the return.
  • We confirm whether the return is approved and send you the return instructions.
  • Send the item back using a trackable delivery service. Please do not send anything back before we confirm.

Return delivery costs

The customer pays the return delivery cost, unless the item arrived damaged, defective or was not what was ordered. Original delivery fees are not refunded for change-of-mind returns.

Damaged, defective or wrong items

If your order arrives damaged, defective or incorrect, contact us within 48 hours of delivery with clear photos of the item and its packaging. We will offer a replacement or a full refund, including delivery fees, at no extra cost to you.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect the returned item, we let you know whether the refund is approved. Approved refunds are made to the original payment method within 7 to 10 business days. Your bank or card provider may take additional time to show the refund.

Exchanges

If you would like to exchange an item, contact us and we will arrange it subject to availability.

Order cancellation

You can cancel an order free of charge at any time before it has been dispatched. After dispatch, the return process above applies.
