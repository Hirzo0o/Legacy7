Shipping Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

  • Orders are prepared within 1 to 3 business days.
  • Delivery within Jordan usually takes 2 to 5 business days after dispatch.
  • International delivery usually takes 7 to 14 business days after dispatch.
  • You see the delivery fee and confirm it before you pay.

Order processing

Once your order is confirmed, we prepare and pack it within 1 to 3 business days. Business days are Sunday to Thursday, excluding public holidays in Jordan.

Delivery times

Delivery times are estimates and start from the day your order is dispatched. Deliveries can take longer during holidays, sales periods or because of customs and courier delays outside our control.

Delivery fees

The delivery fee depends on your location. We tell you the exact fee when you place your order, and nothing is charged until you have confirmed the total.

Customs and import duties

For orders shipped outside Jordan, any customs duties, taxes or import fees charged by the destination country are the customer's responsibility.

Packaging

Every piece is shipped in a reinforced box to protect the frame in transit.

Tracking your order

To check the status of your order, message us on WhatsApp with the name and phone number used for the order.

Wrong address or failed delivery

Please check your address carefully. If a parcel is returned to us because the address was wrong or nobody was available to receive it, we will contact you to arrange redelivery, and a second delivery fee may apply.

Damaged or missing parcels

If your order arrives damaged, please follow the steps in our Return and refund policy. If your parcel has not arrived within the estimated time, contact us and we will follow up with the courier.
